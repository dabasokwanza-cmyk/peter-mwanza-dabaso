ABOUT PETER

A local perspective on local priorities.

Peter Mwanza is presented in the supplied campaign material as a public-service and community leader with more than six years of experience spanning development work, business and civic engagement.

His profile emphasizes practical engagement with communities across Dabaso Ward and attention to livelihoods connected to agriculture, fishing, quarrying, honey production, trade and tourism.

Dabaso

  • Coconut and makuti produce aggregation and market access
  • Farmer cooperatives and support for fishing enterprises
  • Water, health, Kirepwe access and youth skills programmes

Mkenge

  • Road and market improvements
  • ECD classroom development at Mlodo Mwomu
  • Agriculture, women's groups, water and land documentation

Mida

  • Sustainable fishing and mangrove conservation
  • Community–forest stakeholder relations
  • Quarry-worker organization and conservation benefits

Mijomboni

  • Arabuko-Sokoke access corridor
  • Market and fish-marketing improvements
  • Honey value chains and wildlife-conflict follow-up
